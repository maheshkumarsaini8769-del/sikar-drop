Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about packaged water orders, delivery timelines, bulk pricing, and depot policies in Sikar.

We do not supply 20L water campers. Sikar Drop operates under a strict Zero 20L Camper Policy. Local 20L camper jars are frequently subjected to manual hose refilling, unwashed containers, and airborne bacterial contamination. We strictly supply 100% factory-sealed bottled mineral water (250ml, 500ml, 1L, 2L petis) and branded beverages with tamper-evident caps and laser batch stamps.
For retail homes, coaching hostels, and small offices, our minimum order quantity is just 1 Peti. For wholesale mandi pricing, orders begin at 5 Petis. If you require bulk supplies for a marriage banquet (20+ petis), you unlock our highest commercial discount slab with dedicated vehicle delivery.
With our dual hubs on Piprali Road and Nawalgarh Road, standard delivery reaches Piprali coaching corridor, Station Road, Nawalgarh bypass, and Bajaj Gram within 40 minutes. For marriage lawns requiring urgent mid-function top-ups, emergency tempos are dispatched within 25 minutes.

  • Packaged Mineral Water: Bisleri, Kinley, Aquafina, Bailley (250ml, 500ml, 1L, 2L petis).
  • Cold Drinks: Coca-Cola, Thums Up, Sprite, Fanta, Pepsi, Mountain Dew, Limca.
