## Users

- **Event & Wedding Organizers in Sikar:** Families, tent/catering managers, and function organizers booking bulk crates/cartons of sealed water bottles, cold drinks, juices, and beverage cans for marriages, ring ceremonies, parties, and community events across Sikar.
- **Retailers, Cafes, Restaurants & Coaching Hub Canteens:** Shopkeepers, canteens, hostels, offices, and food outlets across Sikar (Piprali Road, Nawalgarh Road, Station Road, Fatehpur Road, Rani Sati Road) ordering regular wholesale stock replenishments by the crate/peti.
- **Households & Offices:** Residents and local offices ordering sealed beverage cartons/packs for guests, summer stock, and daily packaged consumption.

## Product Purpose

Sikar Drop is a local beverage supply and distribution service in Sikar, Rajasthan specializing exclusively in factory-sealed packaged beverages: packaged drinking water bottles (200ml, 250ml, 500ml, 1L, 2L cases), carbonated cold drinks (bottles & cans), fruit juices, and energy/beverage cans. Success means visitors immediately understand what Sikar Drop supplies (and that 20L water jars/campers are NOT supplied), can build a crate/carton ("peti") order across categories in seconds, and dispatch their inquiry or order directly via WhatsApp or phone call.

## Positioning

Sikar's dedicated factory-sealed beverage carton & crate supplier — built solely around sealed PET water bottles, cold drinks, fruit juices, and beverage cans for events, shops, coaching hubs, and bulk buyers, with zero 20L jar/camper handling so logistics stay fast, hygienic, and carton-focused.

## Operating Context

Customers in Sikar order beverages in cartons/petis and crates (e.g., 24x250ml event water cases, 12x1L water cases, 24-can trays, 2L party packs, mixed juice/cold-drink crates), often urgently before an event or shop restock, and finalize quantities and delivery location (Piprali Road, Nawalgarh Road, Station Road, Fatehpur Road, etc.) over WhatsApp or phone call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Offerings:** Packaged drinking water bottles (cases/petis across event & retail sizes), cold drinks (carbonated soft drinks), packaged juices, and beverage cans.
- **Strict Negative Constraint:** Sikar Drop **does NOT supply 20L water jars / campers** ("hum water jar supply nahi karte"). This distinction must be unmistakably clear to prevent irrelevant refill/jar calls.
- **Ordering Mechanism:** Interactive crate/peti order builder that compiles selected beverage categories, pack sizes, customer segment (Event/Shaadi, Shop/Wholesale, Home/Office), and Sikar delivery locality into a structured WhatsApp order message plus direct call triggers.

## Product Principles

1. **Sealed-Pack Clarity First:** Make it unmistakable within seconds that Sikar Drop supplies factory-sealed water bottles, cold drinks, juices, and cans in cartons/crates — and never 20L open/refill water jars.
2. **Peti & Crate Native Ordering:** Speak the real language of Sikar beverage buyers (peti, crate, tray, ml/litre pack sizes, wedding/shop/coaching bulk dispatch) rather than generic single-bottle e-commerce cart metaphors.
3. **Zero-Friction WhatsApp Dispatch:** Turn browsing into a ready-to-send, structured WhatsApp order slip with locality and delivery timing in under 30 seconds.
4. **Local Sikar Trust & Speed:** Ground every touchpoint in Sikar's actual neighborhoods and use cases (coaching hub canteens on Piprali/Nawalgarh Road, marriage gardens, retail counters).
