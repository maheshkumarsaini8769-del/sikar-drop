Sikar Drop was established in 2024 with a singular objective: eliminating the health risks and unhygienic practices associated with unsealed 20L plastic camper jars across Sikar. We made a strict commitment: We strictly do not supply 20L plastic water camper jars in Sikar. Instead, we established direct distribution partnerships with leading national beverage brands including Bisleri, Kinley, Coca-Cola, Thums Up, Sprite, Bailey, and Real Fruit Power to deliver 100% genuine factory-sealed cases and crates with unbroken manufacturer batch seals intact.
