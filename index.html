About Sikar Drop

Sikar's dedicated B2B beverage distribution hub. Providing 100% factory-sealed bottled water cases, cold drink crates, and fruit juice trays directly to marriage gardens, coaching canteens, hostels, and kirana stores.

Our Mission & Strict Zero-Camper Philosophy

Sikar Drop was established in 2024 with a singular objective: eliminating the health risks and unhygienic practices associated with unsealed 20L plastic camper jars across Sikar. Most marriage gardens, student hostels, and events were reliant on reusable campers that lacked batch seals, FSSAI water quality testing, and proper sanitization standards.

We made a strict commitment: We strictly do not supply 20L plastic water camper jars in Sikar. Instead, we established direct distribution partnerships with leading national beverage brands including Bisleri, Kinley, Coca-Cola, Thums Up, Sprite, Bailey, and Real Fruit Power to deliver 100% genuine factory-sealed cases and crates with unbroken manufacturer batch seals intact.
