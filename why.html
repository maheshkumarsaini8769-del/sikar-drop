Our Strict Zero 20L Camper Policy

We do not supply 20L water campers. When we established Sikar Drop, we took a firm decision never to trade in local 20L campers. While campers may offer temporary convenience, multiple independent laboratory investigations in Rajasthan have highlighted severe microbial contamination and heavy plastic degradation in repeated-use jars.

By supplying exclusively 100% factory-sealed bottles and petis from authorized brands (Bisleri, Kinley, Aquafina, Bailley) and commercial beverages (Coke, Pepsi, Sprite), we protect your guests, students, and family with guaranteed hydration integrity.
