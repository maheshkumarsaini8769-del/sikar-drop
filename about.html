About Sikar Drop Wholesale Depot

Sikar's most trusted centralized supply hub for 100% factory-sealed packaged mineral water petis and commercial beverage crates.

Our Story & Origin

Bringing Guaranteed Hydration Purity to Sikar

Sikar Drop was founded with a single mission: to eliminate contaminated, unsealed water supplies from Sikar city. For years, coaching institutes, hostels, wedding banquet halls, and retail merchants had no reliable wholesale source for authentic packaged drinking water.

By establishing our central depot on Piprali Road and an auxiliary hub on Nawalgarh Road, we created a direct logistics corridor that connects factory bottling lines directly to Sikar's commercial counters and wedding banquets.

Institutional Philosophy

Strict Zero 20L Camper Policy

We do not supply 20L water campers. Local 20-litre unsealed campers are frequently refilled with untreated borewell water in unsterilized containers without batch codes or quality testing.

Every single bottle supplied by Sikar Drop is factory-sealed at certified plants (Bisleri, Kinley, Aquafina, Bailley) with tamper-evident caps, laser batch stamps, and BIS/FSSAI licensing.

The Sikar Drop Standard

100% Factory Sealed

Every peti arrives in shrink-wrapped corrugated cartons straight from the bottling plant. Zero intermediate handling or open refilling.
